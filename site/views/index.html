UOR in three steps, in platform words

  1. Canonicalize

    canonical form: the one agreed way to write something, so everyone fingerprints the same thing.

    On your platform: the exact bytes a digest is taken over.

  2. Fingerprint

    content address: a fingerprint of the bytes: same bytes, same name, anywhere.

    On your platform: digest, as in image@sha256:....

  3. Address

    kappa-label: the written form of that fingerprint.

    On your platform: algorithm:hex, such as sha256:<64 hex digits>.
